Covering GW1, GW2, GW3, GW4, GW5 · 20 clubs · shots from fbref, expected goals from the FPL API, goals from the results file the handicap app keeps. Every penalty attempt costs 0.75 xG. Attack · Defence · Net · Player shots board → Defcon board →

Read this first

Three measures each way. Attack is shots taken, penalty-adjusted xG and goals scored. Defence is the same three conceded. Net is attack minus defence — shot difference, xG difference and goal difference — the one view that says who is winning the matches rather than just busy in them.

How the ranking works. Within a gameweek each measure is ranked across the twenty clubs — most is best on attack and net, fewest is best on defence — with ties sharing the mean rank. The three ranks are added and the sum re-ranked 1 to 20: that is the cell on each Composite ranking tab. The other tabs show the raw number with its own rank in brackets. Under every cell is the opponent and whether the club was at home (H) or away (A); hover for the full breakdown.

Avg and Total. The tables open sorted on Avg, the mean composite rank. Every club has played every gameweek so far, so Total — the ranks summed — puts them in the same order; the two would only part company after a blank gameweek, when Total charges the missed week last place and Avg does not.

Where each number comes from. Shots are fbref's, summed by club inside each cumulative sheet and then differenced week to week, so a player who changed club leaves his shots with the club he took them for. xG is FPL's, summed by the club each player played for in that fixture — not FPL's club column, which is where he plays now and would hand the movers' early-season chances to their new clubs (6 player-weeks so far, listed in the audit). Goals come from the results file, not fbref: fbref counts goals by players, so it misses own goals — 7 of the 141 so far, each one the difference between fbref and the score in its match.

Penalties. The rule the player boards use: every attempt costs the club that took it 0.75 xG in that gameweek, and so comes off its opponent's xG conceded too. Weeks it applied are marked with a small red p on the xG tabs. Goals are left as scored.

Conceded is the opponent's for. xG and goals are per match, so that is exact. fbref's shots are only per gameweek, so if an opponent ever plays twice in one gameweek its weekly shots cannot be split between the two matches, and that club's shots conceded will show a blank rather than a wrong number. No club has had a double or a blank yet.
