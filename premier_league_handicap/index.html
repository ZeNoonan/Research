The Handicap Season

Before the season, every club was assigned a handicap — bonus points added to its final total, biggest for the sides expected to struggle. This page re-scores the finished 2025-2026 season on that basis: the adjusted table, how the race unfolded game by game, and how well the handicaps actually levelled the field.

How the handicap works

Season totals

Adjusted points = actual points + handicap. A team with 54 actual points and a 52-point handicap finishes on 106 adjusted points.

Game by game

The handicap is spread evenly across all 38 games (handicap ÷ 38). With a 38-point handicap that is +1 per game: a win is worth 3 + 1 = 4, a draw 1 + 1 = 2. The per-game totals sum back exactly to actual + handicap.

Who beat their handicap?

If the handicaps had been set perfectly, every team would have finished on the same adjusted total — the league average of points. Bars show each team’s distance from that mark: right of the line means the team outperformed its handicap, left means it fell short.
