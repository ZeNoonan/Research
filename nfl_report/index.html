How the system works

Five binary factors each vote +1 (home), −1 (away) or 0. Their sum is the System #; the model bets home at +3 or more, away at −3 or less, and otherwise passes.

  1. Power / over-reaction — back the team the betting line has moved against relative to slow-moving power ratings; line moves overshoot.
  2. Turnover, home (LGT) — back a home team that gave the ball away last game; turnovers are mostly luck and the line over-corrects.
  3. Turnover, away — the same logic for the away team.
  4. Hunger, home (STDC) — back a home team that has been failing to cover; bookmakers like every team to cover about half the time.
  5. Hunger, away — the same logic for the away team.
