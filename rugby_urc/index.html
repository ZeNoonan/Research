The five factors

Each votes +1 for the home side, −1 for the away side or 0. Their sum is the System #; the system bets home at +3 or more and away at −3 or less, and passes otherwise.

#Factor Votes home when…
1Power / over-reactionthe handicap makes home a bigger underdog than the ratings imply
2Turnover — homehome lost the turnover count last time out
3Turnover — awayaway won the turnover count last time out
4Hunger — homehome has been failing to cover
5Hunger — awayaway has been covering
Built by build_site.py from the CSVs in data/. Handicaps are from the home side's point of view: negative means home favoured. 16 clubs, 18 regular rounds.
